## What are the most common problems or complaints you hear from colleagues, team leads, or admins about hybrid working?
- Lose a lot of time on commute days
- Lot harder to find people to ask questions to (big problem)
- Everyone can get shy coming in now because they're not guaranteed a desk.

## What impact do those issues have on productivity, morale, or trust in the process and how do you usually solve it?
- Everyone's feeling a little bit shy
- Definitely has impacted workplace relationships
- Harder to get to know your team 
- Makes the process of working just a little bit slower as people aren't as comfortable with their teams as they should be

## How do exceptions get handled today, for example last-minute cancellations, no-shows, double bookings, visitors, leadership requests, or accessibility needs, and which of those scenarios create the biggest operational risk?
- People just get frustated with each other (big problem)
- Biggest operational risk is the wasted resource and administration time (since room booking is very manual, would like to automate)

## How do managers currently monitor attendance, team collaboration, and workload, and what challenges do you anticipate when employees are working in different locations? 
- There is no current system at the moment
- Stakeholder would like to see a system to see who's coming and out because they rely on teams chats right now.
- Coheson issue, people are a bit more shy, easy to ignore someone on teams, harder to ignore them when they're leaning over your desk. 

## What security, privacy, or legal concerns do you have around storing and managing booking data, especially in relation to employee personal data, access control, auditability, and who should be allowed to see or change bookings?
- GDPR rules are already followed a bit, but they can do with more GDPR training
- The database is not as secure as it could be
- It would be best if people could see what desks/rooms were being booked but not who booked them, allows for privacy (unless it is themself or someone in admin).

### Accessibility issues in specific
- Mobility issues
- Screen readers built in
- Client specific rooms (priority booking)
